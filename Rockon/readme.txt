# RockOn (WoW 1.12.1 Vanilla)

A lightweight AoE group and raid invite addon for World of Warcraft 1.12.1 (Vanilla). It scans nearby friendly players, invites them to your party or raid.
## Features
* **Smart AoE Invites:** Targets nearby friendly players and invites everyone not already in your group or raid.
* **Party Mode:** Invites up to 5 players and stops automatically without converting to a raid.
* **Raid Mode:** Automatically converts your party into a raid as soon as the first player joins and promotes connected members to Raid Assistant.
* **Modifier Support:** Use one command for both modes via Shift
* **Anti-Spam Yell:** Yells once every 5 seconds while spamming the invite button.

## Commands
* `/rockon` — **Normal click/press:** Party invite | **Shift + click/press:** Raid invite.
* `/rockonparty` — AoE invite for a standard 5-man party only.
* `/rockonraid` — AoE invite that converts to a raid and grants assistant to members.
